These photos are NUCLEO-only oscilloscope evidence. They do not approve any
CN8 cable, power-board, 24 V, Gate, OUTx, BOOTx, high-side Vgs, motor, MCSDK,
FOC, or deadtime claim unless the corresponding experiment table says so.
